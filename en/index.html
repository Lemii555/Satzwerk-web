What Satzwerk records

  • Weight and reps per set, or the duration for timed exercises. Warm-up sets are their own rows and never count in any analysis.
  • A rep target per exercise, for example 8 to 12, and a muscle group. A library of 223 exercises, and you can add your own.
  • With Pro, the RIR on the last set of an exercise and the gym of each session.
  • Body: weight, from Apple Health if you want. With Pro, body fat from skinfolds or a scale, and circumferences.
  • A workout counts in the analysis once it is finished.

Findings, not instructions.

Satzwerk shows you where you stand. It does not tell you what to do. No recommendations, no weight targets, no verdict on a lighter week. An onboarding does not know about your herniated disc, your blood pressure or your ankle. A personal plan comes from people who know you. The numbers come from here.

Questions and answers

The app

What is Satzwerk?

Satzwerk is a training log for the iPhone. You enter weight and reps per set and check it off. Satzwerk Pro turns that into whether your reps are on target, how many working sets each muscle group gets and whether you're getting stronger per exercise. With Pro you also enter the RIR on the last set of an exercise, which gives the intensity. There is also a body section with weight, a goal per phase, calories per day, body fat from skinfolds and circumferences.

Who is the app for?

For people who train seriously and want to know whether they’re getting stronger, from their first plan of their own to their tenth year of training. If you’re starting out, pick an entry-level template in the plan library. If you’ve trained longer, Satzwerk Pro shows you progression per exercise, sets per muscle and intensity. How to weigh and measure yourself correctly is explained on its own page in the app. The app gives no recommendations. A personal plan comes from a coach.

Does Satzwerk have a social feed?

No. Satzwerk has no feed, no followers and no likes. You see no other users, and nobody sees your training. Sharing only happens when you pass on a workout as an image yourself.

Is there an Android or Apple Watch version?

No. Satzwerk is available for the iPhone only, not for Android and not for the Apple Watch. The Live Activity on the lock screen runs on the iPhone.

Why is the app called Satzwerk?

The name is made of the German words Satz and Werk. Satz means set, the smallest unit in strength training. Werk means craft, the work you do on your own body.

Pro and price

What is in Free, what is in Pro?

Logging workouts and weight needs no Pro; the analysis is Satzwerk Pro: in the German App Store €4.99 a month or €29.99 a year (as of November 2026). Free and Pro compared

How does the trial month work?

Satzwerk gives you 30 days of Pro. They start with your first finished workout and end on their own. Nothing is taken out, you pay nothing and have nothing to cancel. After that you decide whether to subscribe to Pro.

How do I cancel Satzwerk Pro?

Satzwerk Pro runs through your Apple ID. You can cancel at any time in the iPhone settings under your name > Subscriptions, and switch between yearly and monthly there too. The subscription renews automatically until you cancel it. When Pro ends, nothing is deleted: your workouts, the archive, backup and export stay. The analysis is locked again, and body fat and circumferences can no longer be logged.

I already use gymLog. What changes?

If you downloaded the app before version 2.0, as gymLog, Satzwerk Pro is on us until 31 October 2027. After that, Free applies, or you subscribe to Pro. Your data stays as it is either way.

Your data

Do I need an account?

No, Satzwerk needs no account. The app runs without sign-up, without an e-mail address and without a password. Satzwerk Pro needs none either: the subscription is tied to your Apple ID, and on a new iPhone you get it back with “Restore Purchases”.

Where is my data stored?

Your data lives on your iPhone, with Pro as well. Satzwerk asks once whether to also keep a backup file in your own iCloud. Without your yes there is no file, and you can switch it in your profile at any time. Satzwerk has no server of its own that workouts or body values are sent to. You can export a file at any time, and the history has no time limit.

Does Satzwerk read data from Apple Health?

Only your body weight, including earlier weigh-ins, for example from your scale. Reading is off until you turn it on in your profile. If you want, Satzwerk writes your finished workout, your weight and your body fat percentage to Apple Health, each with its own switch. Your sets stay in Satzwerk.

Can I export my data?

Yes, with or without Pro. Satzwerk exports all workouts and body values as a file that you can back up or move to a new iPhone. Satzwerk reads the same file back in. Workouts and body values can also be exported as CSV. The workout CSV has one row per checked set with date, day, exercise, type, set number, warm-up or working set, weight, reps and time.

Training and plans

Are there templates for training plans?

Yes. The plan library has twelve templates for the gym, for home with dumbbells and for home without equipment, each as an entry-level and an advanced version. The entry-level templates train your whole body. If you don’t have a plan yet, Satzwerk asks on first launch where and for how long you’ve been training and shows the matching templates. They are a starting point, not a recommendation: you take over a copy and change it as you like. You create your own plans yourself or paste them in as text.

Can an AI write my plan for Satzwerk?

Yes. Under “Paste plan” there is a prompt to copy. You give it to an AI together with your plan, and it writes the plan so that Satzwerk can take it over. You paste the result under “Paste plan”.

Can I share a workout?

Yes. You share a finished workout as a story or as a sticker for your own photo. The image shows name, date, duration, exercises and sets, and with Satzwerk Pro your progression too. Body values are never in it. A plan goes on as text, and your training partner pastes it into Satzwerk.

Are there widgets?

Yes, four for your Home Screen. “Today” and “Training” show the day's workout and switch to the new day at midnight. “Week grid” shows 25 weeks of training, “Grid” workouts and weigh-ins.

What is RIR and how is it recorded?

RIR stands for reps in reserve, the repetitions you could still have done at the end of a set. With Satzwerk Pro you enter the RIR on the last set of an exercise: failure, 1, 2 or 3. For the sets before it, Satzwerk estimates the RIR, one more rep in reserve per set. From that, Satzwerk calculates the share of your sets that were close to the limit.

What counts as a set per muscle group?

Satzwerk counts every working set of the week per muscle group, Monday to Sunday, and shows last week next to it. Warm-up sets never count, and RIR plays no part here. A set only counts as hard for intensity: there, sets with RIR 0 to 2 count as hard.
