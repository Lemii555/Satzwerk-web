About Satzwerk

As of November 6, 2026

Satzwerk is a training log with analysis for the iPhone. For people who train seriously and want to know whether they’re getting stronger.

How it started

Satzwerk started as a note. Sets and weights, nothing else. Then body values came in. Then the analysis, because otherwise everything had to be copied into a spreadsheet every time to see whether there was any progress at all.

There was no app that did exactly that and nothing else. So Satzwerk was built. First as a tool for its maker's own training, then for people who train the same way.

From gymLog to Satzwerk

In July 2026 the app went live on the App Store under the name gymLog. The name has a problem: many training apps are called that. Anyone searching the App Store for gymLog easily ends up with a different app.

So in August 2026 the decision for a new name was made. The website and the brand have been called Satzwerk since then, and on the App Store the app carries it from version 2.0. Satzwerk is made of the German words Satz and Werk. Satz means set, the smallest unit in strength training. Werk means craft, the work you do on your own body. Hence the line you see everywhere: Handwerk am eigenen Körper, craft on your own body.

What Satzwerk sticks to

  • Templates, not recommendations. The plan library offers templates as a starting point. An onboarding does not know about your herniated disc, your blood pressure or your ankle. A personal plan comes from people who know you.
  • Findings, not instructions. Satzwerk says “on target” or “too light”. You decide which weight comes next.
  • Your data lives on your iPhone and, if you want, in your own iCloud. No account needed, no Satzwerk server, export as a file at any time.
  • Only numbers that mean something. No tonnage as a finding, no feed. Warm-up sets never count.
  • A week runs Monday to Sunday. Everywhere in the app, for training and for body values.
  • A plan is a plan. What you change during a workout or in the week does not change your plan.
