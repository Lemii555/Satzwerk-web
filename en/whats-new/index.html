What’s New

This is what each version of Satzwerk brought, newest first. Older sections describe the app as it was then. What applies today is on the home page.

3.0

  • Your week as a card stack

    See your week as a stack of cards, with today’s workout in front. Tap a card to see its exercises and start the workout.

  • Card designs

    Give every workout its own design, on the card, during the workout and in the widget. Two designs are free, all seven come with Satzwerk Pro.

  • Share your workout

    Share a finished workout as a story or as a sticker for your own photo. With Satzwerk Pro, your progression is part of the image.

  • Two numbers for every workout

    Open a workout to see the exercises on target and the exercises where you got stronger, and on each exercise whether it was too light or too heavy last time. All of it comes with Satzwerk Pro.

  • Widgets in card design

    Add “Today” or “Training” to your Home Screen. They show the day’s card, tinted too, and switch to the new day at midnight.

  • Progression Pro

    Read one verdict per exercise, stronger, same or weaker, with the two sets below. From the second training week.

  • Edit week

    Plan this week and next: move workouts or add one on a rest day with the plus. Your plan itself stays as it is. Mark a week without training as a training break from the week’s menu.

  • Plan library

    Pick a template for gym, dumbbells or no equipment, for beginners or advanced, and take it as a copy you can change freely.

  • Prompt for your plan

    Copy a prompt under “Paste plan” that lets an AI write your plan so Satzwerk can take it over.

  • Weight from Apple Health

    Bring in your weight from Apple Health, including earlier weigh-ins, for example from your scale.

  • Backup in iCloud

    Back up your data to your own iCloud. Satzwerk asks once, and you can change it in your profile anytime. A new iPhone restores the backup on first launch.

2.1

  • Widgets

    Satzwerk on your Home Screen: Today, Week, Week grid and Grid. Light, dark or tinted, just like your system.

  • Weight

    The change against last week now has a color, based on your goal weight. Without a goal, no color.

2.0.1

  • Satzwerk Pro

    From this version on, the analysis and the body section are part of Satzwerk Pro, monthly or yearly. If you already used gymLog, Pro is on us until 31 October 2027. New here? Then it’s 30 days.

2.0

  • Satzwerk

    The app is now called Satzwerk. Your data stays exactly where it is.

  • Analysis, not just a log Pro

    Progression, sets per muscle and intensity tell you what your numbers mean. A finding, not an instruction.

  • Progression Pro

    Per exercise: did your reps land in the target, and is your heaviest set up against last week.

  • Edit the week

    Drag a workout to another weekday, for this week only. Your plan stays untouched.

  • Archive

    A finished session belongs to itself. Deleting or renaming a plan no longer touches your history.

  • Body Pro

    Body fat from three skinfolds (Jackson & Pollock), weight as a weekly average, measurements with their own breakdown.

  • Satzwerk Pro

    The analysis is new and runs on a subscription, monthly or yearly. 30 days are on us. Anyone who already used gymLog gets a whole year of Pro on top.
